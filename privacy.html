Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the website runledger.site collects, why, and what you can do about it. This page covers the public website, not any future hosted Team service. The open-source CLI and self-hosted team server can run on infrastructure you control without product telemetry.

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is Claudiu Cojocaru, a natural person based in Constanța, Romania, who builds RunLedger. You can reach me at hello@runledger.site about anything in this policy.

What we collect and why

1. The early-access form

  • Data: your email address, the agent you picked (optional) and the text you wrote about what you want to solve (optional).
  • Purpose: to contact you about early access to the hosted RunLedger plans and to understand what people need.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened.
  • Who processes it: the form is sent to Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States), which stores the submission and forwards it to our inbox. This means your data is transferred outside the European Economic Area. Formspree's privacy policy describes the safeguards it uses for these transfers.
  • How long: until early access is over or you ask us to delete it, and in any case no longer than 24 months after your last contact with us.

If the form cannot be sent, the page opens your own email app instead. In that case the message goes from your mail provider to ours, like any other email.

2. Emails you send us

If you email hello@runledger.site, we keep your message and address to answer you (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete correspondence we no longer need, and in any case within 24 months of the last message, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

3. Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

  • What: we count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It records the page, the referring site, the browser and operating system, the screen size and the country, plus a few events such as clicks on the install commands or the GitHub link.
  • What it does not do: it sets no cookies, and it never stores your IP address or full User-Agent. To tell a new visit from a reload, it keeps a random identifier in memory for up to 8 hours. The data is stored as aggregate counts, so it cannot be traced back to you.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how many people use the site and which pages help them (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Opt out: blocking gc.zgo.at or using a content blocker stops it. The site works the same without it.

4. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). When you load a page, GitHub receives your IP address and request details, as any web server does, and may keep them in its logs for security and operations. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the site securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Things we do not use

  • No cookies. The site stores one setting, your light or dark theme choice, in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • No third-party fonts or advertising. The fonts are served from runledger.site itself.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing, and no automated decisions about you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@runledger.site and we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP). You can also contact the authority where you live or work.

The RunLedger software

The open-source CLI, guard and self-hosted team server can process your agent sessions on systems you control and do not send product telemetry. Data can leave your machine when you choose to push a receipt to a team server, configure Slack or public HTTPS webhook notifications, run the GitHub Action, or opt in to --ai or --review (which send selected redacted session information to the Anthropic API). Receipts mask known secret patterns but should still be treated as potentially sensitive. Self-hosted servers store data in SQLite; operators can enable automatic retention using RUNLEDGER_RETENTION_DAYS. Details are in the privacy section of the README and self-hosting guide. A separate privacy policy will cover the hosted Team service before its launch.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change that affects people on the early-access list, we will also email them.
