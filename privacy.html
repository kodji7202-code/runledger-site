Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the website runledger.site collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the public website, not the open-source software, which you run on systems you control.

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is Claudiu Cojocaru, a natural person based in Constanța, Romania, who builds RunLedger. You can reach me at hello@runledger.site about anything in this policy.

What we collect and why

1. Emails you send us

If you email hello@runledger.site, we keep your message and address to answer you (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete correspondence we no longer need, and in any case within 24 months of the last message, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

2. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). When you load a page, GitHub receives your IP address and request details, as any web server does, and may keep them in its logs for security and operations. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the site securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Things we do not use

  • No analytics and no tracking. The site does not count visits and loads no third-party scripts.
  • No cookies and nothing stored in your browser.
  • No third-party fonts or advertising. The fonts are served from runledger.site itself.
  • No forms. The site does not collect anything you type, and we do not sell or share data for marketing or make automated decisions about you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on our legitimate interest, you can object to that processing at any time (Article 21 GDPR); where we rely on your consent (for example optional emails), you can withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@runledger.site and we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP). You can also contact the authority where you live or work.

The RunLedger software

The open-source CLI, guard and self-hosted team server can process your agent sessions on systems you control and do not send product telemetry. Data can leave your machine when you choose to push a receipt to a team server, configure Slack or public HTTPS webhook notifications, run the GitHub Action, or opt in to --ai or --review (which send selected redacted session information to the Anthropic API). Receipts mask known secret patterns but should still be treated as potentially sensitive. Self-hosted servers store data in SQLite; operators can enable automatic retention using RUNLEDGER_RETENTION_DAYS. Details are in the privacy section of the README and the self-hosting guide.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
